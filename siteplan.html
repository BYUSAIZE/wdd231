Site Name

Selected Name: Chando Clothing Brand
This name was chosen because it represents a modern, distinct fashion identity centered around expression, comfort, and style[cite: 5]. It is memorable, concise, and ideal for branding a contemporary clothing line.
Optional Domain Availability:
chando-clothing.com

Site Purpose

The website provides information about the Chando brand, its clothing collections, featured products, and the ideas behind the brand[cite: 5]. It allows visitors to explore different clothing items such as T-shirts, hoodies, hats, and joggers[cite: 5], creating a simple, attractive, and responsive online presence[cite: 5].

Scenarios

Target audience questions that drive the website's content and structure:

  • What are the latest additions and featured styles in the Chando hoodie and T-shirt collections?
  • How can I find detailed descriptions, sizing, and pricing for specific Chando apparel items?
  • What is the brand story behind Chando, and how can I get in touch with customer support or management?

Color Scheme

The color palette is chosen to reflect a sleek, modern streetwear and fashion aesthetic:

  • Primary Color (#111111 - Charcoal Black): Used for main headers, navigation bars, and strong structural elements.
  • Secondary Color (#E5E5E5 - Soft Off-White/Light Gray): Used for card backgrounds and container layouts.
  • Accent Color (#D97706 - Warm Amber/Bronze): Used for buttons, interactive highlights, and call-to-action elements.
  • Text Color (#333333 - Dark Gray): Used for readable body text across all pages.

Typography

Clean, modern fonts to ensure high readability and align with fashion branding:

  • Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) – Used for all primary and secondary headings to give a bold, modern feel.
  • Body Font: Roboto or Open Sans (Sans-serif) – Used for paragraphs, product descriptions, and general content to ensure optimal legibility.
